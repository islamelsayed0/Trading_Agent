# Disclaimer

This repository documents a personal engineering experiment. It is not investment advice,
not a trading strategy, not a recommendation to buy or sell anything, and not a system
anyone should copy and point at their own money.

Specific things to be clear about:

- The author is not a financial professional and holds no license or registration.
- No performance claim in this repo should be read as a forecast. A short run of a small
  account is statistically meaningless in either direction.
- The account used here was funded with an amount the author was fully prepared to lose,
  and was isolated from all other accounts at the broker level.
- The code and rule files described here are published so that the design can be read and
  criticized, not so that they can be run. They contain no credentials and are not
  runnable as published.
- Automated or agent driven trading may be restricted, may require specific broker
  enrollment, and may carry obligations under the rules of your jurisdiction. Check yours.

If you are here from a hiring context, the parts worth your time are the permission model,
the failure cases, and the incident writeups. The profit and loss is the least interesting
number in the repo and is included only because omitting it would be dishonest.
